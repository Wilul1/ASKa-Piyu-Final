I. General Information 
 
 
Santa Cruz Main Campus.  It is the second largest university campus of 
LSPU which is strategically located four (4) kilometers from the own proper.  
As a key player in education, this campus offers relevant curricular programs 
with almost 9,000 student population in Engineering, Nursing & Allied Health 
courses, Hospitality Management & Tourism, Teacher Education, Industrial 
Technology, Criminal Justice Education, Law, Computer Studies, Arts and Sci-
ences, and Business Management and Accountancy. 
 
Siniloan Host Campus.  It lies on a 33-hectare site that is situated in a 
second class municipality between the plains of Sierra Madre Mountain and 
Laguna de Bay.  It is a center of education serving towns in eastern Laguna and 
the northern part of Quezon Province.  LSPU Siniloan has decided itself to 
meet the needs of its stakeholders under a shifting educational climate particu-
larly in agriculture to which it was originally known. 
 
San Pablo Campus.  This campus is a eighty-nine (89) kilometers from 
Manila, resting on approximately 5 hectare land area.  It takes pride of its dis-
tinctive accomplishments in instruction, research, extension services, linkage 
program, and community involvement in collaboration with other campuses. 
 
Los Baños Campus.  This campus is situated along the shoreline of La-
guna Lake, in the first-class urban municipality, northern slopes of Mt. Maria 
Makiling.  It is sixty-five (65) kilometers from Manila in a 7.5-hectare land area 
with approximately 4,000 student’s population where majority are registered in 
Fishery course & sciences and allied courses.
