3. The professor /instructor shall share with parents the responsibility of 
shaping the student’s future and act towards socially acceptable ends. 
In recognition of this, the professor/instructor shall: 
3.1. establish friendly and cooperative relationships with the parents; 
3.2. provide the parents with information that shall serve the best inter-
ests of their children and to be discreet about information received 
from parents; 
3.3. keep parents informed about the academic progress/ performance 
of their children; and 
3.4. exercise tact and utmost sincerity in informing the parents             
about their children’s academic deficiencies and shortcomings. 
 
4. The teacher as a vanguard of change should be responsible in the devel-
opment of the country and its people.  In recognition of this, profes-
sor/instructor shall: 
4.1. recognize that the University belongs to the people, therefore, 
strives to serve the public with genuine concern and dedication; 
4.2. conduct innovative research, initiate sustainable community exten-
sion activities, and assume leadership roles; and 
4.3. discuss national concerns in the community in order to             
transform the lives of the people especially the poor and the disad-
vantaged. 
 
B. Code of Ethics 
 
The officials, faculty, and personnel of the Laguna State Polytechnic 
University shall employ the Code of Ethics to set appropriate guides for ethical 
conduct.
