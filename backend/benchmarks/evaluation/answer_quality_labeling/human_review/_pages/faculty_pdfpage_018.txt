1. Every faculty, official and other personnel of the University shall con-
stantly uphold the honor and dignity of his/her position in all his/her 
actions and relationships with all the other members of the University. 
In fulfilling these obligations, s/he shall: 
1.1. Accept his/her responsibility to maintain a professional level of 
service. To do this, s/he shall: 
1.1.1. strive to do his/her work to the best of his/her 
knowledge and ability; 
1.1.2. understand the full range of the responsibility of his/her 
position and accept the changes and/or added func-
tions and responsibilities incumbent upon the position; 
1.1.3. demonstrate a willingness to try new and innovative pro-
cedures that will bring about a more efficient and expe-
ditious handling of official matters; and  
1.1.4. seek and acquire professional growth by continuous in-
volvement in advanced studies, researches, extensions, 
conferences, seminars, trainings, workshops and attend-
ance to meetings and assemblies. 
 
1.2. Respect the worth and dignity of each member of the university. 
This means, s/he shall: 
1.2.1. refrain from making derogatory remarks about a col-
league or the school system in general except in official 
relationships where his/her professional opinion is re-
quired; 
1.2.2. conduct professional business through the proper chan-
nels; 
1.2.3. consider another person’s point of view by maintaining 
an open mind and tolerant views; 
1.2.4. formulate policies and decisions only by meritorious con-
siderations. S/he shall not allow himself to be influ-
enced by biased information or of his/her own persua-
sions; 
1.2.5. seek redress of grievances by giving testimony and apply-
ing evidence thereof without malice only to the proper 
authorities;
