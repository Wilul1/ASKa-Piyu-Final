Overview
This service provides assistance for Issuance of Transcript of Records/Transfer Credentials/Certifications/CAV/Authenticated Documents.

Office / Division
Registrar

Who May Avail
All (Walk-In students & Graduates)

Requirements
- Requirement: Clearance, Request form Accounting
  Where to Secure: Office/Office of the Registrar

Steps
1. Client Step: A. For undergraduate students
   Agency Action: Issue request slip (LSPU-REG-SF011) Review and check
   Fees: None
   Processing Time: 5 minutes
   Person Responsible: Registrar’s Staff

2. Client Step: Fill out request slip with clearance (LSPU-REGSF011) and secure signatures of Head of offices B. For graduates - Fill out request slip (LSPUREGSF-011)
   Agency Action: accomplished request of students Assess corresponding fees of requested documents
   Fees: Not specified
   Processing Time: Not specified
   Person Responsible: Not specified

3. Client Step: Pay required fees. Proceed to the Cashier’s office.
   Agency Action: Receives payment and issues official receipt Certificatio ns Copy Grades F CAV Certificate of General Weighted Ave. Subject description SPES Permit Study Authenticat ion nd
   Fees: P75.00/page; P150/page; P30.00/page; P137; Second copy of diploma: P100.00
   Processing Time: 2 minutes
   Person Responsible: Cashier’s Staff

4. Client Step: Submit filled out request slip and official receipt of payment and get the claim stub with date of release
   Agency Action: ⎯ Receives and reviews the presented receipts and request slip ⎯ Schedule the date of release of requested document. Issues claim stub ⎯ Locates the permanent record of student concerned ⎯ Prepares and encodes the requested document ⎯ Reviews and counterchec ks entries on the prepared draft of requested document ⎯ Final printing of requested document ⎯ Affixes college ⎯ seal Signs the document
   Fees: None
   Processing Time: 2 minutes (Inactive files – 1 day) Active files – 15 minutes 25 minutes 5 minutes 1 minute 1 minute 1 minute
   Person Responsible: Registrar’s Staff

5. Client Step: Claims the requested documents on the scheduled data indicated in the claim stub
   Agency Action: Records and releases document
   Fees: Not specified
   Processing Time: 2 minutes Active Files
   Person Responsible: Registrar’s Staff
