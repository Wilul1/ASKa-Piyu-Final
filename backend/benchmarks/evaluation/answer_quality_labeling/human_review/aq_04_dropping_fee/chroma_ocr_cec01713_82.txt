Dropping Grades Policy

Undergraduate Academic Policies > Article 6 > Academic Load and Changes > 4.2 > Dropping Grades Policy

4.2.1. If a subject is dropped after the first day of the midterm ex- aminations, the instructors concerned shall indicate in the form whether or not the student shall be given a grade of “5.0” for the course based on his/her class standing in that subject.
4.2.2. Any student who drops a course without the College Dean's approval shall be given an automatic grade of “5.0” for the course of his/her registration privileges is curtailed or entire- ly drawn.
4.2.3. If a student is dropped from the roll by his/her instructor/ professor due to the Attendance Rule, a student is given a grade of “5.0” and if class standing is below “3.0”.
4.3. A dropping fee of P20/unit is required. (LSPU BOR 1046, s.2012)
