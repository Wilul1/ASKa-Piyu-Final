Sec. 5. Substitution of Courses 
5.1. Substitution Policy. Substitution of course is not allowed unless for 
valid reasons. 
  
5.2. Substitution Guidelines 
5.2.1. Application letter for substitution of course/program is 
made indicating the reasons. 
  
5.2.2. All requests for substitution must be recommended by the 
Program Coordinator/Dean and submitted to the registrar 
for approval before 12% of the regular class meetings have 
been held during the term. Any request submitted and/or 
received after that shall not be considered for the semester. 
  
Sec. 6. Substitution of Subjects

at least one of the following conditions:
