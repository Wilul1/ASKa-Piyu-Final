1. Teaching is a personal commitment of oneself to others with the inten- tion to fulfill these obligations to satisfaction. As such, the professor/ instructor shall:
1.1. exemplify mental, social, emotional and professional maturity where he/she recognizes his/her strengths as well as his/her weaknesses;
1.2. share ideas, instructional resources to fellow teachers especially those which have been found to be effective;
1.3. rise above personal feelings and biases in the interest of group ac- tion and group results; and
1.4. enhance his/her credibility in the classroom through quality teach- ing and adequate preparation.
2. Teaching as a commitment recognizes the centrality of the learner in the educative process. In recognition of this fact, the professor/instructor shall:
2.1. respect learners as person and value him/her as such;
2.2. enhance the learner’s capacity for growth and create the proper climate for that growth;
2.3 accept the learner as s/he is; when s/he shows ignorance, not ridi- cule him/her, when s/he differs, not to reject him/her, and when s/he is proved wrong, not to condemn him/her;
2.4. inspire the best effort from his/her students because their standard of performance is in themselves the motivation to do one’s best;
2.5. deviate from the influence of personal friendship, pressure from vested groups and consider only the achievement of students in giving grades;
2.6. recognize the differences among students and seek to meet their individual needs;
2.7. encourage students to formulate and work for high individual goals of their physical, intellectual, creative, socio-emotional and spiritual endowments; and
2.8. respect the right of every student to withhold confidential infor- mation unless required by authorized agency or by law.
3. The professor /instructor shall share with parents the responsibility of shaping the student’s future and act towards socially acceptable ends.
In recognition of this, the professor/instructor shall:
3.1. establish friendly and cooperative relationships with the parents;
3.2. provide the parents with information that shall serve the best inter- ests of their children and to be discreet about information received from parents;
3.3. keep parents informed about the academic progress/ performance of their children; and
3.4. exercise tact and utmost sincerity in informing the parents about their children’s academic deficiencies and shortcomings.
4. The teacher as a vanguard of change should be responsible in the devel- opment of the country and its people. In recognition of this, profes- sor/instructor shall:
4.1. recognize that the University belongs to the people, therefore, strives to serve the public with genuine concern and dedication;
4.2. conduct innovative research, initiate sustainable community exten- sion activities, and assume leadership roles; and
4.3. discuss national concerns in the community in order to transform the lives of the people especially the poor and the disad- vantaged.
B. Code of Ethics The officials, faculty, and personnel of the Laguna State Polytechnic University shall employ the Code of Ethics to set appropriate guides for ethical conduct.
1. Every faculty, official and other personnel of the University shall con- stantly uphold the honor and dignity of his/her position in all his/her actions and relationships with all the other members of the University.
In fulfilling these obligations, s/he shall:
1.1. Accept his/her responsibility to maintain a professional level of service. To do this, s/he shall:
1.1.1. strive to do his/her work to the best of his/her knowledge and ability;
1.1.2. understand the full range of the responsibility of his/her position and accept the changes and/or added func- tions and responsibilities incumbent upon the position;
1.1.3. demonstrate a willingness to try new and innovative pro- cedures that will bring about a more efficient and expe- ditious handling of official matters; and
1.1.4. seek and acquire professional growth by continuous in- volvement in advanced studies, researches, extensions, conferences, seminars, trainings, workshops and attend- ance to meetings and assemblies.
