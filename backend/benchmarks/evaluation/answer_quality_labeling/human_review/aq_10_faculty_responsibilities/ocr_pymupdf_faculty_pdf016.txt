III. Commitment  
of  the LSPU Faculty 
 
A. Commitment of Faculty 
 
1. Teaching is a personal commitment of oneself to others with the inten-
tion to fulfill these obligations to satisfaction. As such, the professor/
instructor shall: 
1.1. exemplify mental, social, emotional and professional maturity 
where he/she recognizes his/her strengths as well as his/her 
weaknesses; 
1.2. 
share 
ideas, 
instructional 
resources 
to 
fellow 
teachers                 
especially those which have been found to be effective; 
1.3. rise above personal feelings and biases in the interest of group ac-
tion and group results; and 
1.4. enhance his/her credibility in the classroom through quality teach-
ing and adequate preparation. 
 
2. Teaching as a commitment recognizes the centrality of the learner in the 
educative process. In recognition of this fact, the professor/instructor 
shall: 
2.1. respect learners as person and value him/her as such; 
2.2. enhance the learner’s capacity for growth and create the proper 
climate for that growth; 
2.3 accept the learner as s/he is; when s/he shows ignorance, not ridi-
cule him/her, when s/he differs, not to reject him/her, and when 
s/he is proved wrong, not to condemn him/her; 
2.4. inspire the best effort from his/her students because their standard 
of performance is in themselves the motivation to do one’s best; 
2.5. deviate from the influence of personal friendship, pressure from 
vested groups and consider only the achievement of students in 
giving grades;
